📖 Applicable Statutory Rules & Rates

Late Fee Rates: ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) for regular returns with tax liabilities or outward supplies, and ₹20 per day (₹10 CGST + ₹10 SGST) for Nil returns.
Maximum Fee Caps: Per-return maximum limit is capped by previous year turnover: ₹2,000 (up to ₹1.5 Cr), ₹5,000 (₹1.5 Cr–₹5 Cr), or ₹10,000 (above ₹5 Cr). Nil returns are capped at ₹500.
Section 50 Interest: 18% per annum on net cash tax liability paid after the due date (applicable to GSTR-3B only).
