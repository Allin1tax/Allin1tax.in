GST Late Fee Calculator

Work out the late fee and interest for a delayed GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B. Everything you type stays in your browser.

Enter the due date and the filing date to see the late fee.

Rates used: late fee of ₹50 a day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) for a return with tax or supplies, and ₹20 a day (₹10 + ₹10) for a nil return. Maximum late fee per return is ₹2,000, ₹5,000 or ₹10,000 by turnover slab, and ₹500 for a nil return. Interest is 18% a year on tax paid late, under Section 50, and applies to GSTR-3B only.

This is an estimate. The GST portal works out the final late fee when you file, and notifications can change the rules, so confirm on the GST portal before paying. Rates cross-checked with this guide and this one. GSTR-9, GSTR-4 and other returns are not covered here.
